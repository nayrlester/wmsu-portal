PH curriculum tuition uses org-scoped fee schedules (Finance UFMS `/tuition/fee-schedules`) and USiS `POST /enrollments/assess` with schedule-first `prepareAssessment`. UFMS opens AR and materializes `student_installments` from `payment_plan_templates` (exam_period or monthly). Cashier posts allocate FIFO to installments. USiS `verify-payment` passes when UFMS `paidAmount >= minimumDueRequired` per `enrollment_payment_policies` (default first installment when installments exist). Registrar registers after verify; balance remains collectible in UFMS.
