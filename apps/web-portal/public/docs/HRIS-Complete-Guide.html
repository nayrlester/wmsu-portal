1. Introduction

1.1 What is this platform?

The HRIS + Payroll Management System is an integrated human resources and payroll platform built for Philippine companies. It combines employee records, time and attendance, leave, payroll processing, government compliance (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR), recruitment, documents, and approvals in one secure web application—with a dedicated Employee Self-Service (ESS) portal and mobile app for staff.

1.2 Primary purpose

  • Maintain accurate employee and organizational data
  • Track attendance and daily time records (DTR)
  • Process leave requests and approvals
  • Run payroll with statutory deductions and contributions
  • Generate payslips and compliance reports
  • Support hiring through job requisitions and candidate tracking
  • Route HR and payroll requests through supervisor and workflow approvals

1.3 Key benefits

  • Single source of truth for HR and payroll data
  • Role-based access so employees see only what they need
  • Audit trail for sensitive changes (admin users)
  • Philippine payroll rules including government tables and reporting views
  • Self-service reduces HR workload for routine requests

2. Getting Started

2.1 System requirements

Web (Admin & ESS)

  • Modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari (latest two versions)
  • Stable internet connection
  • Screen resolution 1280×720 or higher recommended

Mobile

  • iOS or Android device with the company HRIS mobile app installed
  • Camera and location services if your company requires selfie or geofence clock-in

2.2 Accessing the system

  1. Open the URL provided by your IT or HR department (e.g. https://hris.yourcompany.com).
  2. You will see the Sign in page.

Figure: Figure 2-1: Login page

Figure 2-1. Login page

# Element Description
1 Email address Your work email registered in the system
2 Password Your account password
3 Sign in Submits credentials; redirects based on your role

2.3 Signing in

  1. Enter your email address.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Click Sign in.

After a successful login:

  • Employees are taken to the ESS Home (/ess).
  • Supervisors / managers with request permissions may land on Employee Requests (/requests/coa).
  • HR, payroll, and admin users land on the first module they are allowed to access (often Dashboard).

2.4 Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

If your administrator has enabled MFA on your account:

  1. After email and password, you are prompted for a verification code.
  2. Enter the code from your authenticator app or SMS.
  3. Complete verification to continue.

If you lose access to your MFA device, contact your system administrator.

2.5 Forgot password

Password reset is managed by your company administrator through System Administration → Users. There is no self-service reset on the login screen unless your deployment adds one. Contact HR or IT if you cannot sign in.

2.6 Initial setup (administrators)

New company setup typically follows this order:

  1. Seed organization structure (branches, departments, positions).
  2. Configure pay groups and payroll settings.
  3. Create system users and assign roles.
  4. Link users to employee records where applicable.
  5. Assign supervisors via Reporting Lines.
  6. Communicate ESS login instructions to employees.

2.7 Demo / training access (University Identity)

Training and non-production sign-in uses the University Identity Service (not legacy HRIS-only demo emails). Sign in at the Identity portal, then launch HRIS from the platform launcher.

4. Role-Based Access

Access is controlled by roles and permissions. Your administrator assigns one or more roles per company.

4.1 Super Admin

Typical access: System Administration (Users, Roles & Permissions, Audit Logs), platform and security modules if deployed.

Cannot be assumed for: Day-to-day HR data entry unless also given HR roles.

4.2 Company Admin / HR Manager

Typical access: Dashboard, Organization, Employees, Recruitment, Leave approvals, Documents, Clearance, Analytics.

Common tasks: Hire employees, maintain org structure, approve leave, manage recruitment.

4.3 Payroll Manager / Payroll Officer

Typical access: Payroll dashboard, payroll runs, payslips, adjustments, government compliance, attendance DTR (company view).

Common tasks: Create periods, compute payroll, review entries, approve runs, export reports.

4.4 Department Manager / Supervisor (line_manager)

Typical access: Approval Center — leave, Certificate of Attendance (COA), workflow inbox for direct reports.

Requirement: Employee record linked to user; reporting line from team members to supervisor.

4.5 Employee

Typical access: ESS only — clock, leave, payslips, COA, disputes, profile.

4.6 Auditor

Typical access: Read-only views on reports, analytics, and audit-related screens (as configured).

6.4 Newly Hired status and welcome email

When an employee is hired from recruitment or added without a work email, their status is Newly Hired. They do not have a login account until HR completes onboarding.

Step Who Action
1 HR (recruitment) Hire candidate — employee record created; no welcome email yet
2 HR Open employee → Employment tab
3 HR Enter work email and click Save changes
4 System Creates user account, sends welcome email with invite link
5 System Status changes to Probationary
6 Employee Opens invite link from email → sets password → uses ESS

Employment tab notes:

  • Newly Hired status is system-assigned and read-only until work email is saved.
  • Helper text on the work email field explains that saving will create login and send the welcome email.
  • If email delivery fails, a warning is shown; fix mail settings and save again.

Employee first login: New hires must use the invite link in the welcome email (/invite?token=...), not the standard login page first. After setting a password, they sign in with their work email.

See also: Knowledge base — Send welcome email.

23. Best Practices

HR

  • Keep reporting lines current when managers change.
  • Require supervisor on every active employee.
  • Process COA and leave within SLA so payroll is accurate.
  • Use clearance checklist for all separations.

Payroll

  • Lock attendance before compute.
  • Review adjustments and loans before approving a run.
  • Reconcile government reports against approved payroll each period.
  • Never approve a run until spot-checks are complete.

Security

  • Use unique accounts; do not share passwords.
  • Assign least-privilege roles.
  • Review audit logs periodically.
  • Disable users immediately on separation.

Employees

  • Clock in and out daily; use COA promptly if you miss a punch.
  • File leave early for planned absences.
  • Verify payslips within the dispute window defined by HR.

Administrator Manual — HRIS + Payroll

Audience: HR administrators, company admins, super admins
Version: 1.0 | June 2025

1. Administrator responsibilities

Administrators maintain organizational data, user access, employee records, approvals configuration, and integration with payroll operations. This manual covers admin-console features visible in the live navigation catalog.

2. Access and roles

2.1 Recommended roles

Role code Use for
company_admin Full company HRIS (except platform super-admin tools)
hr_manager Employees, leave, recruitment, attendance
payroll_manager Payroll runs, compliance, payslips
line_manager Approvals only (supervisor) — not full admin
auditor Read-only reporting
super_admin Users, roles, audit logs

2.2 Managing users

Path: /admin/users

  1. Click Add user — enter name, email, password policy-compliant password.
  2. Assign company membership and default company.
  3. Assign roles for that company.
  4. Link employee record when the user is also an employee (required for ESS and supervisor routing).

2.3 Managing roles

Path: /admin/roles

  1. Review system roles (cannot delete system flag roles).
  2. Clone or create custom roles with minimum permissions needed.
  3. Avoid granting super_admin.access to routine HR staff.

Privileged permissions (restrict carefully): user.manage, role.manage, super_admin.access, audit.view.

3. Organization setup

Complete before importing employees:

  1. Branches — legal/work sites; configure geofence if attendance requires it.
  2. Departments & positions — used on employee profiles and recruitment.
  3. Reporting lines — /organization/reporting — every employee must have a supervisor for leave and COA.

4. Employee lifecycle

Stage Action Path
Hire (recruitment) Move candidate to Hired; creates Newly Hired employee /recruitment/jobs
Onboard login Add work email on Employment tab → welcome email sent /employees/{id}
Hire (manual) Add employee without work email /employees/new
Update Transfer, promotion, supervisor change /employees/{id}
Documents Upload 201 file items Employee profile / Documents
Separate Clearance + offboarding /clearance/checklist

Supervisor field: Mandatory on create. Updates take effect immediately for new approval routing.

Employee onboarding (welcome email)

Welcome emails are not sent at hire time. Use this sequence:

  1. Hire from recruitment (or create employee without work email).
  2. Employee status = Newly Hired, work email empty, no user account.
  3. Open Employment tab, enter work email, Save changes.
  4. System provisions account (pending_invite), emails invite link, sets status to Probationary.
  5. Employee accepts invite at /invite, sets password, then uses ESS.

Mail configuration (server): Ensure MAIL_DRIVER, SMTP credentials, MAIL_FROM, and WEB_APP_URL (production web URL for invite links) are set on the API service.

Organization → HR Settings (/organization/hr-settings): Optional company email domain for reference; work email is entered manually per employee on the Employment tab.

5. Approval configuration

Request type Where approved Routing
Leave /leave/approvals Supervisor → HR stages
COA /requests/coa Direct supervisor or request.manage
COE /requests/coe HR / request managers
Payroll disputes /requests/disputes HR / payroll
Workflow tasks /workflows/inbox Per workflow definition

Ensure reporting lines match real managers before enabling self-service COA.

6. Recruitment administration

  1. Configure lookups: /recruitment/settings/departments (and related sections).
  2. Create job openings with department, position, headcount.
  3. Manage candidates and interviews on job detail pages.
  4. Hire candidate — creates employee with status Newly Hired; supervisor and agreed salary required.
  5. Do not expect a welcome email at hire — open the new employee profile, Employment tab, enter work email, and save to send onboarding email (see §4).

7. Documents and compliance

  • Documents: /documents — policies, templates, company files.
  • Clearance: /clearance — exit process templates and checklists.

8. Audit and security

Path: /admin/audit

Filter by date, user, action, entity. Use for investigations and compliance evidence.

Security checklist:

  • Remove access on last working day
  • Review role assignments quarterly
  • Enforce MFA for admin accounts (when enabled)
  • Train supervisors on COA/leave approval only for direct reports

9. Navigation and menu maintenance

Navigation items are seeded from navigation_catalog. After permission or menu changes, run navigation seed in deployment:

npm run dev:seed-navigation --workspace=apps/api

Users see menu items only if they hold the listed permission code.

10. Training environments

Use a dedicated non-production environment for training. Ask your system administrator for training user accounts — do not reuse production credentials.

Part III — Employee Self-Service Guide

Employee User Guide — Self-Service (ESS)

Audience: All employees using web ESS or mobile app
Version: 1.0 | June 2025

1. Overview

Employee Self-Service lets you manage your own time, leave, payslips, and HR requests without visiting the HR office for every task. You need a user account linked to your employee record.

New employees: HR will send a welcome email to your work email after your record is set up. Use the invite link in that email to set your password before using the normal login page.

Web ESS URL: Same as company login — you are redirected to /ess after sign-in.

2. First login (new hires)

If you were just hired:

  1. Wait for HR to add your work email — you will receive a welcome email when they save your profile.
  2. Click the invite link in the email (not the login page first).
  3. Set your password on the invite screen.
  4. You are signed in and taken to ESS.
  5. Later visits: use the company login URL with your work email and password.

See How to accept your invite.

3. ESS home

Figure: ESS Home

The home screen shows:

  • Your name, department, and position
  • Clock status — last punch and whether you are timed in
  • Leave balance summary
  • Shortcuts to Time, Schedule, DTR, Leave, Payslips

4. Time and attendance

3.1 Clock in and clock out

Path: /ess/attendance or mobile Attendance tab

  1. Open the Time screen.
  2. Read the status message (e.g. “You are timed in since 8:02 AM”).
  3. Press Time in when you arrive.
  4. Press Time out when you leave.

If your company requires location or selfie, follow the prompts. Denying permissions may block clocking.

3.2 Daily Time Record (DTR)

Path: /ess/dtr

  1. Select the date range (e.g. current pay period).
  2. Review each day: status, worked hours, late minutes.
  3. If a day is wrong, use Certificate of Attendance (section 4).

3.3 Schedule

Path: /ess/schedule

View your assigned shifts for the week (if scheduling is configured).

5. Certificate of Attendance (COA)

Use when you forgot to time in or out.

Path: /ess/coa | Mobile: Requests → COA

Figure: COA Form

  1. Choose correction type:
    • Missing time-in
    • Missing time-out
    • Missing both
  2. Enter a clear reason (at least 10 characters).
  3. For each affected date:
    • Work date
    • Proposed time-in and/or time-out
    • Optional remarks per date
  4. Click Add date for multiple days in one request.
  5. Submit — status becomes Pending until your supervisor acts.

You can track submissions under My requests (/ess/requests) or the COA My requests tab.

6. Leave

Path: /ess/leave

  1. Check balances for each leave type.
  2. Select leave type, start date, end date.
  3. Add reason if required.
  4. Submit.

You cannot cancel approved leave in ESS — contact HR. Pending requests may be visible to your supervisor immediately.

7. Payslips

Path: /ess/payslips

  1. Open the payslip list (most recent first).
  2. Select a period to view gross, deductions, and net pay.
  3. Download PDF if available.

If something looks wrong, use Payroll Disputes (section 7).

8. Payroll disputes

Path: /ess/disputes

  1. Select the payslip period in question.
  2. Describe the issue (minimum 10 characters).
  3. Submit — HR/payroll reviews under admin Employee Requests → Payroll Disputes.

How to submit a Certificate of Attendance (COA)

Use this when you forgot to time in or time out for one or more workdays.

Before you start

  • You need an active ESS account linked to your employee record.
  • Know the correct dates and approximate times you worked.
  • Your supervisor must be set in the system (HR assigns reporting lines).

Steps (web)

  1. Sign in — you should land on ESS Home.
  2. Open Menu → Certificate of Attendance, or go to /ess/coa.
  3. Select Correction type:
    • Missing time-in — you forgot to clock in.
    • Missing time-out — you forgot to clock out.
    • Missing both — both punches missing for that day.
  4. Enter a Reason (at least 10 characters). Example: “Forgot to clock in after client meeting.”
  5. Under Dates, enter:
    • Work date (YYYY-MM-DD)
    • Proposed time-in and/or time-out as required by the type
  6. Click Add date to include more days in one request.
  7. Click Submit to supervisor.

Steps (mobile)

  1. Open Requests tab → COA.
  2. Choose correction type, date, times, and reason.
  3. Tap Submit COA.

After submission

  • Status shows Pending until your supervisor approves or declines.
  • Track progress under My requests or COA history tab.
  • When approved, your DTR is updated automatically.

title: How to send a welcome email to a new hire category: Employee Management audience: HR Administrator tags: [admin, employees, recruitment, onboarding]

How to send a welcome email to a new hire

Overview

New hires do not receive a welcome email automatically when you click Hire in recruitment. The system creates the employee record first with status Newly Hired and no work email. HR adds the work email later; saving the profile triggers account creation and sends the welcome email with the invite link.

Prerequisites

  • SMTP or mail delivery configured on the server (MAIL_DRIVER, SMTP_*, MAIL_FROM, WEB_APP_URL).
  • Permission employee.update to edit employee profiles.
  • Permission recruitment.manage (or equivalent) to hire from recruitment.

Hire from recruitment

  1. Open Job Requisitions → Job Openings and select a job.
  2. Move the candidate through the pipeline to Hired.
  3. Enter agreed salary and select a supervisor when prompted.
  4. Confirm hire — the system creates an employee record and opens their profile.
  5. You will see a notice: Employee record created. Open their profile and add a work email to send the welcome email.

The new employee appears with:

Field Value after hire
Employment status Newly Hired
Work email Empty
User account Not created yet

Send the welcome email

  1. Open Employee Management → Employees and select the new hire (or follow the redirect after hire).
  2. Open the Employment tab.
  3. Confirm status shows Newly Hired (read-only until onboarding completes).
  4. Enter the employee's work email (this becomes their login username).
  5. Click Save changes.
  6. On success, the system:
    • Creates a user account linked to the employee
    • Sends the welcome email with an invite link to set their password
    • Changes status from Newly Hired to Probationary

If mail delivery fails, you may see a warning that the account was created but the email could not be sent. Fix mail configuration and save again, or reset the invite from User Administration.

New employee: accept invite

  1. Open the welcome email on the work email inbox.
  2. Click the invite link (do not use the general login page first).
  3. Set a password on the /invite page.
  4. After accepting, sign in with work email and the new password.
  5. Employee-only users are routed to Employee Self-Service (/ess).

Manual add employee (without recruitment)

  1. Employee Management → Add employee — leave work email blank on the create form.
  2. Save — status defaults to Newly Hired when no work email is provided.
  3. Open the employee profile → Employment tab → enter work email → Save changes (same as above).

How to accept your invite and sign in

Before you start

  • HR must add your work email on your employee record and save it before you receive a welcome email.
  • Use the email address HR assigned (your work email), not a personal email, unless HR told you otherwise.

Steps

  1. Check your work email inbox for the welcome message from your company.
  2. Click the Set up your account (invite) link in the email.
    • Do not go to the login page first — the invite link contains a one-time token.
  3. On the invite page, enter and confirm your new password.
  4. You are signed in automatically and taken to Employee Self-Service.
  5. For later visits, open the company login URL and sign in with your work email and password.

Troubleshooting

Issue What to do
No email received Ask HR to confirm work email was saved on your Employment tab and mail is configured
Link expired or invalid Ask HR to save your work email again or request a new invite from admin
Lands on admin dashboard Contact HR — your account may have extra roles; ESS users should open /ess
Wrong password at login Use work email, not personal email

How to run payroll

Standard semi-monthly or monthly payroll using the Payroll Runs screen.

Prerequisites

  • payroll.view and payroll.run permissions
  • Attendance and leave for the period are finalized
  • COA and disputes for the period are resolved
  • Payroll period exists for the date range

Steps

  1. Go to Payroll Management → Payroll (/payroll/runs).
  2. Select the payroll period (create one under Settings if missing).
  3. Start or select a payroll run for that period.
  4. Click Compute and wait for completion.
  5. Review the employee register — check gross, deductions, net.
  6. Post any last-minute Adjustments and re-compute if needed.
  7. Click Approve (payroll.approve) when accurate.
  8. Confirm payslips appear in ESS for sample employees.
  9. Export Government Compliance reports for the same period.

Warnings

  • Do not approve with known DTR errors.
  • Keep compute and approve roles separated when possible.

How to view payslips

  1. ESS → Payslips (/ess/payslips) or mobile Payslips tab.
  2. Select a payroll period from the list.
  3. Review earnings and deductions.
  4. Download PDF if the download action is shown.

Payslips appear only after payroll is approved for that period.

title: How to reset a user password category: System Administration audience: Super Admin tags: [admin, security]

How to reset a user password

Permission: super_admin.access / user management

  1. System Administration → Users (/admin/users).
  2. Search for the user by email.
  3. Open the user record.
  4. Set a new temporary password per your security policy.
  5. Communicate the password securely to the user.
  6. Require password change on first login if your policy mandates it.

Disable accounts for separated employees the same day as last working day.
