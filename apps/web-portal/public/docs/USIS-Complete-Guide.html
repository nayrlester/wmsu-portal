1. How you get in

  1. Sign in at WMSU Portal (Unified Access) with your Identity account.
  2. Open the Enrollment (USiS) tile — you must have the enrollment platform assigned.
  3. SSO sends you to USiS; your Identity role decides which portal you see (student vs registrar vs faculty).

2. Roles and what you see

Platform access (enrollment.access) only lets you open USiS. Fine-grained menus come from Identity roles mapped to permission packs:

Identity roleUSiS experience
studentStudent portal only — profile, enrollment, grades, payments, COR, etc. No registrar or faculty menus.
registrarRegistrar portal — applicants, students, enrollments, academic setup, grades administration.
adviserFaculty/advising — advising and limited grade/student reads, not full registrar.
cashierPayments, assessments, clearance reads — not full registrar catalog.
employeeLaunch only unless another role is assigned — no registrar by default.
identity_adminFull USiS permissions when enrollment platform is granted.

Portal Admin shows a short note under each role when editing users. Ask an Identity administrator to change roles or platforms.

3. Student portal

  • Home path: /student after SSO when the student role is present.
  • Self-service: admission status, subject selection, schedule, assessment, payment status, grades, academic history.
  • If you see “No student profile linked,” your Identity account is not yet linked to a student record — contact the Registrar.

4. Registrar and offices

  • Registrar staff land on /registrar with applicants, student records, enrollments, and academic configuration.
  • Administrative office routes (Admissions, OSA, Guidance, Health, Scholarship) require the matching permission pack — not every staff user sees every office.

6. Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to check
USiS tile missingenrollment platform not assigned in Portal Admin
Registrar menus as a studentUser should have student role only; remove registrar/staff roles
403 on registrar APIsRole pack does not include that permission — expected for students
SSO errorSign out of Portal, sign in again; confirm Base API and Identity API are running

6. Reports & dashboards

  • Registrar: /registrar home and /reports/dashboard API for role-aware cards.
  • Student: dashboard under /student — enrollment status, grades, payments (no registrar list APIs).
  • GET /api/v1/enrollment/reports/dashboard — requires registrar or report permissions from Identity pack.
