UFMS — University Financial Management System

Finance module (WMSU CIS) — TOR VI.E.3

  • Web (local): http://127.0.0.1:5182
  • Platform code: finance
  • API: http://127.0.0.1:3500/api/v1/finance

Launch

Sign in at WMSU Portal and open Finance (UFMS). You need the finance platform plus an Identity role that maps to the right permission pack (e.g. cashier for POS and payment reads, finance staff roles for budget/procurement/accounting).

Modules

  • Budget — appropriations and monitoring
  • Accounting — GL, JEV, trial balance, withholding, bank reconciliation (sidebar under Accounting)
  • Procurement — purchase requests and PO workflows
  • POS / Cashiering — point-of-sale and collections

University Accounting is part of UFMS Finance — there is no separate accounting launcher tile.

Roles

finance.access (platform) allows launch only. Menus and APIs follow Identity role packs — assign cashier, registrar (limited reads), or identity_admin as appropriate in Portal Admin.
